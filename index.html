Three engines, working together

Each does one job well. You don't have to think about them, but you can choose.

Xray-core

The tunnel itself: VLESS with REALITY, VMess, Trojan and Shadowsocks over TCP, WebSocket, gRPC, HTTPUpgrade and XHTTP, plus routing and DNS.

Zeptun TUN

A light, fast packet engine that captures the whole device in TUN mode and hands its traffic to Xray. The default, and lighter on memory and battery than the core's own engine.

Aether free

A free tunnel through Cloudflare WARP or MASQUE, with optional Tor. No server, account or subscription needed. Pick an exit country if you like.

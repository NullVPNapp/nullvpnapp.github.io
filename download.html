macOS

macOS 12 or newer · Apple silicon and Intel
Your device
Universal disk image
NullVPN-macos-universal.dmg ·
Download

The app isn't signed by Apple yet. Drag it to Applications, then run this once in Terminal before opening it:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/NullVPN.app

Updates installed from inside the app don't need it again.

iPhone & iPad

iOS 15 or newer
Your device
Unsigned app, for sideloading
NullVPN-ios-unsigned.ipa ·
Download

This file has to be signed before it installs, with a certificate whose profile includes the Network Extensions capability (for example with ESign or Sideloadly). Without that capability the app installs but can't connect. On iOS 18 and later, add NullVPN to Control Center to connect without opening the app.

Check your download

Each release lists the SHA-256 of every file in SHA256SUMS.txt. Compare it with yours:

Windows (PowerShell)

Get-FileHash .\NullVPN-windows-x64-setup.exe

Linux / macOS

sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt --ignore-missing
